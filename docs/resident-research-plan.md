# Alameda County resident research protocol

Recruit at least 30 residents across North County, Oakland/Alameda, Central County, Tri-City, Tri-Valley, and unincorporated communities. Include at least ten Spanish-preferring residents and at least eight residents with disability, older-adult, mobility, medication, or transportation-access needs.

Each moderated session covers: address match confirmation, ambiguity, ZIP fallback, expired result, partial source outage, distinction between mapped findings and local-plan context, three actions, text alternative to the map, source verification, privacy understanding, and emergency-service boundaries.

Do not collect real medical diagnoses. Use public/civic address fixtures unless a participant explicitly chooses their own address after the privacy explanation. Store notes under participant IDs, not addresses.

Success criteria:

- Residents can correctly identify the confirmed jurisdiction and whether a finding is mapped, plan context, unknown, or unavailable.
- No participant interprets the product as a live alert or combined property-risk score.
- English and professionally reviewed Spanish users can recover from ambiguity and failure without facilitator help.
- Keyboard, screen-reader, 200% zoom, mobility, medication, and no-car scenarios each produce actionable findings for the product backlog.
