# Alameda County resident research protocol

## Initial English-only public beta

Before the limited public beta, complete at least eight moderated English
sessions across at least four of these six county regions: North County,
Oakland/Alameda, Central County, Tri-City, Tri-Valley, and unincorporated
communities.

At least three participants must bring an access need relevant to the product,
such as disability, older-adult, mobility, medication, or transportation access.
Spanish-language participation is not a requirement for this English-only
release.

Each session covers address confirmation, ambiguity, ZIP fallback, expired
results, partial source outage, the distinction between mapped findings and
draft/adopted local-plan context, three actions, the map text alternative,
source verification, privacy understanding, and emergency-service boundaries.

The beta research gate requires:

- Eight completed English sessions.
- Four county regions represented.
- Three access-needs sessions.
- A documented findings report and named research owner.
- Zero unresolved critical comprehension or safety findings.

Do not collect real medical diagnoses. Use public/civic address fixtures unless
a participant explicitly chooses their own address after the privacy
explanation. Store notes under participant IDs, not addresses.

## Later language-access and broader research milestone

Retain the broader 30-session study across all six county regions for the future
language-access/general-availability program. That later study includes at least
ten Spanish-preferring residents and at least eight residents with relevant
access needs. Spanish sessions begin only after the complete critical journey
has professional translation and in-context safety review.

Later language releases must separately verify that residents can recover from
ambiguity and failure in the released language without facilitator help.
